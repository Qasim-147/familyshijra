    How to add your branch

    Anyone in the family can contribute. Nothing goes live until the family admin has checked it.

    1. Find your place

      Search for your father, mother or grandparent in the tree and open their card.

    2. Send your details

      Tap Add relative, choose how you are related, and add names, dates, cities and a photo.

    3. The admin approves

      The family admin checks each entry, corrects it if needed, and publishes it to the shijra.
